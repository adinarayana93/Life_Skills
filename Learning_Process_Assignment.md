# Learning Process Assignment

## Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a topic by explaining it in simple words as if you are teaching someone else.

## Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

* **Focused (Active) Mode:** When we concentrate fully on one task or concept to understand details and solve problems.
* **Diffuse Mode:** A relaxed thinking mode that helps us connect ideas, understand difficult concepts, and solve problems after taking a break.

**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. Deconstruct the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.

# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

**Paraphrase (summarize) the Grit video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit means staying passionate and persistent toward a long-term goal, even when things become difficult.

## Question 2

**Paraphrase (summarize) the Introduction to Growth Mindset video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

A growth mindset means believing that our skills and intelligence can improve through effort, challenges, mistakes, and feedback.

## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and effort can influence our results.

The key point is to focus on what we can control instead of blaming external factors.

## Question 4

**What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?**

- Believe you can improve.
- Focus on learning, not just results.
- Put in effort.
- Accept challenges.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Use feedback to improve.
- Take responsibility for your actions and outcomes.

## Question 5

**What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

- Take full responsibility for my learning.
- Stay with a problem until I understand it.
- Put in more effort when something is difficult.
- Never write code I do not understand.
- Treat challenges, confusion, discomfort, and errors as learning opportunities.
- Use documentation and online resources before asking for help.
- Take ownership of my projects.
- Focus on mastery instead of just completion.
- Make my code work, readable, modular, and efficient.
- Relax, focus, understand, code, and repeat when solving problems.

# Focus Management

## Question 1

### What is Deep Work?

Deep Work means focusing on a difficult task without distractions. It helps me think deeply, learn faster, and produce better work.

## Question 2

### According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Set aside a fixed time for deep work.
- Avoid distractions during that time.
- Start with at least 1 hour and gradually increase it.
- Keep social media, unnecessary internet use, and emails away while working.
- Create a daily deep work routine.
- Use an evening shutdown routine to plan the next day.
- Keep distraction periods separate from focused work.

## Question 3

### How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Set a fixed time every day for focused work.
- Keep my phone and unnecessary notifications away.
- Work on one important task at a time.
- Take planned breaks instead of checking distractions whenever I feel like it.
- Spend some time walking, sitting quietly, or meditating to give my brain a break.
- At the end of the day, review unfinished tasks and plan the next day.
- When a difficult task feels scary or boring, start with just 30 seconds and gradually continue.

## Question 4

### What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- It can increase anxiety.
- It makes me compare myself with others.
- It can disturb my peace of mind.
- It can reduce productivity.
- It can make me less present in real life.
- It can become a major source of distraction.

# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1

### What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted sexual comments, jokes, or remarks.
- Unwanted touching or physical contact.
- Sexual messages, images, gestures, or requests.
- Repeated unwanted attention or advances.
- Any sexual behaviour that makes someone feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected.

## Question 2

### What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- Stay calm and make it clear that the behaviour is not acceptable, if it is safe to do so.
- Keep a record of what happened, including relevant messages or other evidence.
- Report the incident to the appropriate person or the company's POSH/complaints committee.
- If I witness it, I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
- I would not ignore repeated incidents or spread the matter unnecessarily.

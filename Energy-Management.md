# Energy Management

## Question 1

**What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?**

- Listening to music
- Going for a walk
- Spending time with friends and family
- Watching movies
- Taking a break and doing nothing for some time

## Question 2

**When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?**

- When I have too many tasks to complete.
- When I face a difficult problem for a long time.
- When deadlines are close.
- When I am learning something completely new.

## Question 3

**How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?**

- I feel energetic and highly engaged.
- I enjoy what I am doing.
- I feel motivated to keep going.
- I can focus easily on the task.

## Question 4

**Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation.**

- Good sleep supports learning and memory.
- Deep sleep helps the brain retain information.
- Lack of sleep reduces learning ability and focus.
- Enough sleep supports heart health and the immune system.
- Regular sleep and wake times improve sleep quality.
- A cool room can help with better sleep.
- Avoid alcohol and caffeine close to bedtime.

## Question 5

**What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?**

- Sleep and wake up at the same time every day.
- Keep the bedroom cool.
- Follow a regular sleep routine.
- Avoid caffeine and alcohol close to bedtime.
- If I cannot sleep, leave the bed and return when I feel sleepy.

## Question 6

**Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points.**

- Exercise helps create new cells in the hippocampus.
- It improves long-term memory.
- Regular exercise improves attention and focus.
- Exercise helps improve mood.
- Regular exercise strengthens the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex.
- Exercise can help protect the brain from diseases.
- Around 30 minutes of cardio, 3–4 times a week, is suggested.

## Question 7

**What are some steps you can take to exercise more?**

- Set a fixed time for exercise.
- Start with small and manageable workouts.
- Exercise 3–4 times a week.
- Choose activities I enjoy, such as walking, running, or sports.
- Track my workouts to stay consistent.
- Slowly increase the duration or intensity.
